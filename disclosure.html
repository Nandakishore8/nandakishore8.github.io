2. Nature of Business

Nanda Financial Services is a Mutual Fund Distributor registered with AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India). Our primary business activities include:

  • Distribution of mutual fund schemes
  • Portfolio review and analysis services
  • Investment advisory services
  • Financial planning and guidance

3. Commission and Fee Structure

Mutual Fund Distribution Commission

We may receive commission from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) for mutual fund distribution. These commissions are:

  • Paid by AMCs directly, not by investors
  • Based on the scheme's exit load and other factors
  • Typically in the range of 0.5% to 1.5% of invested amount (varies by scheme)
  • Received at the time of investment and periodically as trailing commission

Advisory Fees

For portfolio review and advisory services, we may charge fees directly to clients. Fee structures vary based on portfolio complexity and services provided.

No Hidden Charges

We do not charge any hidden fees or unauthorized charges. All fees and commissions are disclosed to clients before engagement.

4. Potential Conflicts of Interest

We acknowledge the following potential conflicts of interest:

  • Commission Bias: We may recommend funds that provide higher commission to us
  • AMC Relationships: We may have business relationships with certain AMCs
  • Product Promotion: We may promote certain schemes more actively

How We Manage Conflicts: We prioritize client interests, provide transparent information, and allow clients to make independent decisions.

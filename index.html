Direct vs Regular Plans

Which model fits your situation?

Aspect Direct Plans Regular Plans
Distributor commission Not included Built into the plan structure
Expense ratio Generally lower Generally higher (includes distribution costs)
Ongoing support Self-managed Guidance from a distributor, if you use one
Portfolio assistance Self-directed Reviews and help choosing or consolidating, if offered
Transaction assistance You transact via your own platform A distributor can help you execute
Best suited for Hands-on investors comfortable managing alone Investors who value ongoing support

There is no universally better option. The right choice depends on the value of ongoing support relative to its cost — and both plans invest in the same underlying scheme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you share anything with us

What exactly do you check?

Six dimensions: fund overlap, diversification, risk concentration, costs, goal alignment and overall portfolio complexity. You get a structured write-up, then a discussion in simple language.

Is the Portfolio X-Ray really free?

Yes — the initial review is completely free. No charges, no credit card, no “premium version” later. As AMFI-registered mutual fund distributors, we may earn commission if you choose to invest through us afterwards — but you are never obligated to.

Do I need to transfer my mutual funds?

No. Nothing moves. The review is analysis only — your funds stay exactly where they are unless you personally decide otherwise.

Do I have to invest through you?

No. Many people take the review and nothing more. There is no obligation to invest through us, and we will never pressure you to do so.

How do you earn?

We are AMFI-registered mutual fund distributors (ARN-332725). If you choose to invest through us, we may earn commission built into the plan. The review itself is free and comes with no obligation. You can verify our ARN on the AMFI India website.

Will you recommend switching funds?

Only if the analysis clearly supports it. Any observations are explained with reasons, and the decision to act — or not act — is always yours.

Do you guarantee returns?

No. No one can guarantee returns on mutual fund investments. The Portfolio X-Ray is about understanding what you own — not predicting markets.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Your details are used only to prepare and deliver your review. We don’t sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. See our Privacy Policy for the specifics.

How long does the review take?

Submitting the form takes under 2 minutes. You’ll typically hear from us within 1 working day, and the full review discussion usually takes about 20–30 minutes.

What information do I need to provide?

Just two quick questions to start — your portfolio range and your goal. Fund names, folio numbers or your CAS statement are only needed if you choose to share them during the review. The more you share, the deeper the analysis.

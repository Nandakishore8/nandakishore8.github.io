2. Nature of Business

Nanda Financial Services is a Mutual Fund Distributor registered with AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India). Our primary business activities include:

  • Distribution of mutual fund schemes
  • Portfolio review and analysis services
  • Investment guidance and execution support
  • Investor education and awareness

3. Commission and Fee Structure

Mutual Fund Distribution Commission

As an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor, we receive trail commission from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) for facilitating mutual fund investments in regular plans. These commissions are:

  • Paid by AMCs directly out of scheme management expenses (TER), not billed separately to investors
  • Regulated under SEBI guidelines with full transparency
  • Received as trailing commission based on the net asset value (NAV) over the investment holding period
  • Detailed scheme-wise commission ranges are shared with investors as required by AMFI guidelines

Portfolio Review Services

Our initial portfolio reviews and health evaluations are provided free of cost to help investors understand their asset allocation, overlap, and diversification. We do not charge separate advisory fees for mutual fund distribution.

No Hidden Charges

We do not charge any hidden fees, platform entry fees, or unauthorized transaction charges.

4. Conflict of Interest Management

In accordance with the AMFI Code of Conduct, we disclose that mutual fund distribution generates commission from AMCs. We manage potential conflicts of interest by:

  • Placing investor suitability and long-term financial goals at the forefront of every portfolio review
  • Providing objective analysis of fund overlap, category concentration, and cost efficiency
  • Ensuring that investors retain complete autonomy over all investment and transaction decisions
  • Adhering strictly to AMFI and SEBI codes of conduct for mutual fund intermediaries

5. Code of Conduct
